# Refund Policy

## Subscription refunds

You can request a refund within 14 days of a new subscription or renewal charge. Contact support from the email address on the account and include the invoice number. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds are not available for unused time after the 14-day request window, except where required by applicable law. Usage-based charges are non-refundable after they have been incurred.

## Refund request process

To request a refund, open the Help center and select Contact support. Include the account email, invoice number, charge date, and a short explanation. Do not include full payment-card numbers or passwords.

## Renewal charges

Renewal charges are eligible for review during the 14-day refund window. If a renewal was unexpected, cancel the subscription to prevent the next renewal while the request is reviewed.

## Processing and payment methods

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank processing times vary, and a refund may take longer for international cards. The support team will provide a confirmation reference when the refund is approved.

## Exceptions

Refund exceptions may be considered for duplicate charges, service outages, or charges made after a documented cancellation request. Exceptions are reviewed individually and are not guaranteed.
