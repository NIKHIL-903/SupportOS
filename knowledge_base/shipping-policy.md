# Shipping Policy

## Standard delivery

Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after an order ships. A tracking link is sent by email when the package leaves the warehouse.

## Express delivery

Express domestic delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days after an order ships. Express shipping is available for eligible products at checkout.

## International delivery

International delivery usually takes 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for customs duties and import taxes unless the checkout page says otherwise.

## Address changes

Contact support as soon as possible if you need to change a shipping address. An address cannot be changed after the order has been handed to the carrier.

## Tracking and delivery status

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to appear after shipment. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support with the order number so the delivery team can investigate.

## Split shipments

Items in the same order may ship separately when they are stored at different warehouses. Each package receives its own tracking link, and shipping charges are not duplicated for split shipments.

## Delayed packages

Weather, carrier disruptions, customs inspections, and incorrect addresses can delay delivery. Support can investigate a package after the carrier's estimated delivery date has passed.

## Damaged packages

Photograph the package and the damaged item, then contact support within 7 days of delivery. Keep the original packaging until the support team confirms whether a return is required.

## Delivery address rules

We cannot deliver to parcel lockers for every product category. International customers should verify that the recipient name and phone number meet local carrier requirements before placing an order.
