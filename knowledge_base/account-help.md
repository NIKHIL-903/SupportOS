# Account Help

## Password reset

To reset your password, open the Sign in page and select **Forgot password?**. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the form. We will send a password-reset link to that address. The link expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Locked accounts

An account is temporarily locked after several unsuccessful sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes before trying again. If the account remains locked, use the password-reset flow or contact support from the email address registered to the account.

## Updating your email

Open Settings, choose Account, and select Change email. Confirm the new address using the verification email before signing out.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification can be enabled from Settings > Security. You can use an authenticator application or recovery codes. Store recovery codes somewhere secure because support cannot display them after they are generated.

## Account deletion

To delete an account, open Settings > Account and select Delete account. Account deletion is permanent. Export any projects or files you need before confirming the request.

## Contacting support

When contacting support about account access, use the email address associated with the account. Never send a password, authentication code, or recovery code in a support message.
